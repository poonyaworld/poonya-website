Privacy

Effective date: May 7, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Poonya may collect, use, and protect information when you use the app or contact us.

Information We May Collect

We may collect information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, contact messages, group participation details, and app preferences. We may also collect basic usage information needed to operate, secure, and improve the app.

How We Use Information

Information may be used to provide app features, personalize your daily experience, support User Groups, respond to messages, improve reliability, prevent misuse, and communicate important updates.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share limited information with service providers that help operate the app, when required by law, or when necessary to protect Poonya, our users, or the public.

User Groups

When you join or create a User Group, certain profile, progress, or group activity details may be visible to other members of that group, depending on the app's settings and features.

Data Retention

We keep information only as long as reasonably needed for app operation, user support, legal compliance, and legitimate business purposes.

Children

Poonya is not intended for children under 13. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 13 without appropriate consent, we will take steps to delete it.

Your Choices

You may contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of personal information where applicable. Some data may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate operational reasons.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect information, but no system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent through the contact form.
