Daily Compass for mindful living

Poonya

Poonya helps you turn small, intentional actions into daily momentum. Each morning begins with a Daily Spark, followed by four simple seeds of change across Self, Others, Neighborhood, and Earth. It is a calm, practical companion for growing well-being, kindness, community, and environmental care, one day at a time.

The four circles of impact

A daily rhythm that feels simple, warm, and doable.

In a world that can often feel overwhelming, it is easy to wonder if one person's choices truly matter. Poonya is built on the Daily Compass: a holistic approach to living that shows how small, intentional actions create a ripple effect far beyond yourself. WHAT YOU CAN DO • Start each day with a Daily Spark and four curated do-good habits • Complete habits, earn points, and track your daily goal and streak • See your progress over time with charts and impact insights • Save favorite habits and quotes for quick access • Reflect with journal entries on quotes that move you • Discover new habits, create your own, and add personal notes when you complete them • Join groups with friends, family, or colleagues to build habits together • Chat, share habits and quotes, and celebrate progress as a team THE FOUR CIRCLES OF IMPACT Self (Well-being): The center. You cannot pour from an empty cup. Nourishing your mind and body creates the foundation for everything else. Others (Kindness): The spark. A simple gesture or mindful word can transform someone's day and build human connection. Neighborhood (Community): The foundation. Strengthening local bonds creates a resilient, supportive ecosystem for everyone. Earth (Environment): The legacy. Small acts of stewardship help ensure the world we leave behind is as vibrant as the one we inherited. BUILT FOR REAL LIFE Poonya's Daily Compass is a holistic rhythm—not a rigid checklist. Your four daily seeds are your contribution to a better world, one manageable day at a time. Goodness was never meant to be a solo journey. With Groups, you can invite others, stay accountable, and turn individual ripples into a wave of positive change. At the heart of Poonya is Punya—the idea that every mindful choice is a seed. Plant them today, nourish them together, and watch the world bloom. Poonya is free to use. An optional one-time in-app purchase unlocks higher group limits. Small Acts. Big Change. Together.
